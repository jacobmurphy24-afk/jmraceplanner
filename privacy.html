Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Training Planner (jmraceplanner.co.uk) helps you plan races and training and analyse the activities you record on your sports watch. This policy explains what data the site uses, where it is kept, who else handles it and what your rights are. It’s written to be read: if anything is unclear, get in touch.

Who we are

The site is run by Jacob Murphy, an individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: privacy@jmraceplanner.co.uk.

The site is free. It has no adverts, no analytics or tracking scripts, and your data is never sold or used for marketing.

The short version

  • Your activities and health data stay on your device. Activity files, routes, heart rate, sleep and similar data are stored in your browser on the device you uploaded them on. They are not stored on our servers.
  • Your account and plans are stored with Google Firebase in London (europe-west2), so you can sign in on any device and share plans.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted at any time.

Health data

Heart rate, sleep, heart-rate variability and training data count as health data under UK data protection law, which needs extra care. We use it only to give you the analysis you ask for, and only with your explicit consent. Before anything is read from a data export or activity file, the site explains what it uses and asks you to agree by ticking a box. We save a record of when you agreed with your account (or on the device, if you don’t have one), and ask again if what we do with the data changes. Most of the data never leaves your device. The parts saved to your account (thresholds and block review summaries) are visible only to you. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Activity analysis → Withdraw consent: this deletes the activity data on that device and the consent record. To have the data saved to your account deleted as well, delete your account (Plans & sharing → Delete my account).

Legal basis

  • Contract: to provide the service you sign up for (your account, plans and sharing).
  • Explicit consent: for health data, as above.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the site secure and prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many invitation emails can be sent).

Uploading your activity data

When you upload a data export from your watch’s app (a ZIP) or individual .fit activity files, they are opened and read entirely in your browser. Only activity files, activity names, and daily sleep and resting heart rate are read; everything else in the export (profile, devices, gear, settings and so on) is ignored. Nothing from the file is sent to our servers.

The site doesn’t connect to your watch maker’s account. If we offer a direct connection in future, this policy will be updated before it is switched on, explaining what is stored and where.

Storage on your device

The site uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in (Firebase), to store the activity data you add, to remember small preferences, and to work offline (a service worker keeps a copy of the site’s files). There are no advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Signing out deletes your activity data and the offline copy of your plans from that device. You can also use Settings → Activity analysis → Clear stored activities, or clear the site’s data in your browser.

How long we keep it

  • Account, plans, analysis settings and reviews: until you delete them, or delete your account (Plans & sharing → Delete my account), which deletes them straight away.
  • Shared plans: a plan you own is deleted with your account; your name and email are removed from other people’s plans.
  • Invitation counts: 2 days.
  • Hosting providers’ technical logs: according to their own policies, typically a few days to a few weeks.
  • Data on your device: until you clear it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it’s used, and receive a copy in a portable format. You can withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself at any time in Plans & sharing → Delete my account. To do any of the others, email privacy@jmraceplanner.co.uk; we’ll reply within one month. You can export your plans yourself at any time with Save plan.

If you’re unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113), though we’d appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Access rules on the database let only you read your own analysis data, and only a plan’s members read that plan. Passwords are handled by Google and never seen by us.

Children

The site is not intended for children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
